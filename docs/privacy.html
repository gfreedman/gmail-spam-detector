Privacy policy

Gmail Spam Detector (OAuth app name “Spam Detector Runner”) is a personal tool. It is used by one person, on their own Gmail account, and is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission it reads, labels and deletes spam in that owner's Gmail, and writes logs and archived copies of deleted spam to the owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheets.

What it does with the data

Nothing leaves the owner's Google account. No data is sold, shared, sent to third parties or used for advertising. The OAuth login exists only so the owner can start a run of the script from their own computer.
